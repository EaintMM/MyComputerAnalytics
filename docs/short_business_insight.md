# Business Insights

> These insights are based on the synthetic portfolio dataset and do
> not represent the actual business's performance.

## 1. Overall Performance

- Revenue: 692.46M MMK
- Gross Profit: 156.06M MMK
- Gross Margin: 22.54%

## 2. Category Performance

The Computer category generates the largest revenue contribution,
while Accessories has a substantially higher gross margin.

## 3. Branch Performance

TGI branch generates higher revenue than LLM branch in the synthetic dataset,
while their gross margins are relatively similar.

## 4. Product Performance

Revenue leaders and gross-profit leaders are not identical,
demonstrating why both metrics should be monitored.

## 5. Inventory

Inventory value is concentrated in the Computer category.
Several products also show high historical stock coverage,
which should be investigated rather than automatically classified
as excess inventory.
